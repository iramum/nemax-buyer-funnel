What is inside

Seven reasons the published yield may not match what you receive.

Each reason follows the same order: what happens, what to check, and what to do about it.

01The yield is based on asking prices, not real sales.
02Different sources give different yields for the same area.
03The yield does not include costs.
04The rent is from a renewal, not a new tenancy.
05The average rests on a few buildings.
06Prices and rents are both changing.
07Your cash is not ready on transfer day.
The number in the listing

Is not the number in registered data

In Dubai Silicon Oasis, listings showed 8.23% in the first half of 2026. Registered data for August 2026 showed 6.69% for the same community.

Sourced and dated

Every figure carries its source

Bayut, Property Monitor, Engel & Völkers, a dataset derived from Dubai Land Department records, Cavendish Maxwell and Savills. Where two sources disagree, both numbers are printed.

These are third-party market figures, published between July and October 2026. Past results, not a promise, and not a NEMAX return.
