What is inside

Seven reasons the published yield may not match the real one.

Each reason follows the same order: what happens, what to check, and what to do about it.

01The yield is based on asking prices, not real sales.
02Different sources give different yields for the same area.
03The yield does not include costs.
04The rent is from a renewal, not a new tenancy.
05The average rests on a few buildings.
06Prices and rents are both changing.
07The cash is not ready on transfer day.
Listing and fact

Side by side, area by area

For each of the 14 communities, the map prints the listing figure next to the figure from signed contracts, so the gap is visible at once.

Sourced and dated

Every figure carries its source

Portal listings, market reports and data derived from Dubai Land Department records. Each source and date sits in a footnote inside the map. Where two sources disagree, both numbers are printed.

These are third-party market figures, published between July and November 2026. Past results, not a promise, and not a NEMAX return.
